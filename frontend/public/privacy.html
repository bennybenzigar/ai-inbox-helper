Privacy Policy — AI Inbox Helper

Last Updated: October 29, 2026

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance:
AI Inbox Helper's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Introduction

AI Inbox Helper ("we", "our", or "the application") is a recruitment productivity platform that assists recruiters in categorizing candidate emails, drafting suggested replies, monitoring recruitment pipeline bottlenecks, and analyzing attendance patterns. We are committed to protecting your privacy and maintaining the security of any data you share with us.

This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, how we store and protect it, and how you can control your data. By using AI Inbox Helper, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Access and Collect

When you choose to connect your Google account or upload data into AI Inbox Helper, we collect and process the following information:

  • Google Account Profile & Email: When you authenticate via Google OAuth 2.0, we access your email address and basic profile information under the openid and email scopes to identify your connected mailbox.
  • Gmail Messages & Metadata: Under the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly scope, we access incoming candidate emails (sender name, sender email, subject, timestamp, body text, and attachment presence) for the sole purpose of displaying candidate inquiries in your inbox and classifying their intent.
  • Recruiter-Uploaded Timesheets and Pipeline CSVs: Work hours, leave records, and candidate board statuses uploaded to the Attendance Pattern Checker and Stall Alerter modules. These files are processed client-side and stored in our database for your review.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to provide and improve the recruitment workflows within the application:

  • Intent Classification: Incoming candidate emails are categorized into 6 operational intents (Interview Scheduling, Application Status, Salary & Compensation, Document Requests, Job Details, and Other / Unclear) using Google Gemini AI.
  • AI Response Drafting: We pass candidate message text to Google Gemini AI (gemini-2.5-flash) to generate concise, professional reply suggestions for your review.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Review: AI drafts are displayed for human recruiters to review, edit, approve, or reject. No email is ever sent automatically without explicit recruiter approval.
  • Attendance Analysis: Uploaded timesheets are analyzed to identify attendance patterns and flag irregularities for recruiter review.
  • Pipeline Monitoring: Candidate board data is analyzed to detect stalled recruitment stages and alert recruiters to bottlenecks.

4. Google Limited Use Disclosure

In accordance with Google's Limited Use requirements, AI Inbox Helper:

  • DOES NOT use Google user data (including email content, metadata, or profile information) to train generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.
  • DOES NOT sell, rent, lease, or trade Google user data to data brokers, advertising networks, information resellers, or any third parties.
  • DOES NOT use or transfer Google user data for serving personalized, retargeted, or interest-based advertisements.
  • DOES NOT allow humans to read your private email messages unless: (a) you explicitly approve a message in the review interface, (b) you provide explicit written consent for technical troubleshooting, or (c) it is required by applicable law.
  • LIMITS its use of Google user data to providing and improving the user-facing features described in this policy. All access is proportional to what is necessary to deliver the functionality requested by the user.

5. Third-Party Services

AI Inbox Helper integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Google OAuth 2.0: For user authentication and Gmail access authorization.
  • Google Gmail API: For reading candidate emails from your connected Gmail account (read-only access).
  • Google Gemini AI: For intent classification and reply draft generation. Email content is sent to Google Gemini's API for processing; Gemini does not store or retain this data beyond the API request lifecycle.
  • Render.com: For hosting the application frontend and backend. Data is stored in Render-managed PostgreSQL databases with encryption at rest.

6. Data Storage, Security & Retention

All data is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS). Application data is stored securely in a managed PostgreSQL database hosted on Render.com with encryption at rest. Google OAuth refresh tokens are stored in the server's restricted connection metadata store and are never exposed to client-side scripts, logs, or third parties.

We retain your data only for as long as you maintain an active connection. When you disconnect your Gmail account or perform a factory reset, all associated data is permanently deleted from our database.

7. Data Deletion and Revocation of Access

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Disconnect Gmail: Navigate to the Gmail Connection page within the application and click "Disconnect" at any time. This immediately deletes your stored OAuth tokens from our database and stops all Gmail synchronization.
  • Revoke via Google Account: You can revoke AI Inbox Helper's access at any time via your Google Account Third-party apps settings.
  • Factory Reset / Data Purge: Go to Settings > Data Management and select "Factory reset" to permanently and irreversibly delete all messages, drafts, audit logs, timesheets, candidate alerts, and OAuth tokens from our database.
  • Request Data Deletion: You may also request complete data deletion by emailing us at the contact address below. We will process deletion requests within 7 business days.

8. Children's Privacy

AI Inbox Helper is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the application after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
