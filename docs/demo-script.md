# Demo Walkthrough Script (Loom Video)

A comprehensive walkthrough script covering all modules, AI workflows, and features of the **AI Inbox Helper**.

---

### Segment 1: Executive Overview & Architecture (0:00 – 0:45)
- **Problem Statement:** Recruiters receive high volumes of candidate inquiries across disparate topics, leading to delayed follow-ups, inconsistent messaging, and candidates stalling in pipeline stages.
- **Solution Overview:** Introduce AI Inbox Helper — combines Gemini AI intent classification, human-in-the-loop draft generation, live Gmail mailbox synchronization, sent email tracking (Outbox), timesheet compliance checking (Attendance), and pipeline velocity tracking (Stall Alerter).
- **Tech Stack Highlights:** Show live deployment on Render, Angular 22 Signals-first frontend, Node.js/Express backend with `Node16` resolution, and PostgreSQL database.

---

### Segment 2: Dashboard & Intent Analytics (0:45 – 1:30)
- **Live Metrics:** Walk through total candidate messages, pending drafts requiring review, approved drafts, and attention-required counts.
- **Intent Breakdown:** Highlight the 6 intent distribution bars (`Interview Scheduling`, `Application Status`, `Salary & Compensation`, `Document Requests`, `Job Details`, `Other / Unclear`).
- **Real-Time Activity Stream:** Show real-time SSE audit feed updating as candidate events occur.

---

### Segment 3: Candidate Inbox & Gemini AI Drafting (1:30 – 2:45)
- **Search & Filters:** Demonstrate 350ms debounced search, filtering by intent category, and status filters.
- **AI Draft Suggestion:** Open a candidate inquiry; showcase the context-aware reply draft generated by Google Gemini (`gemini-2.5-flash`).
- **Inline Editing & Review:** Modify the draft text in the inline editor, regenerate alternative suggestions, approve the draft, and demonstrate the human-in-the-loop review policy (no autonomous sending).
- **Pagination & Bulk Actions:** Demonstrate rows-per-page selection (5, 10, 25, 50, 100), multi-select checkboxes, and bulk deletion with modal confirmation.

---

### Segment 4: Live Gmail Integration & Auto-Sync (2:45 – 3:30)
- **OAuth Connection:** Navigate to `/gmail-connection` showing connected status with authorized test accounts.
- **Background Auto-Sync:** Explain the 5-minute server-side daemon timer that pulls new emails automatically.
- **On-Demand "⟳ Sync Gmail":** Trigger an instant mailbox pull directly from the Inbox toolbar; show the live sync timestamp updating.
- **Live Reply Dispatch:** Send an approved draft directly through the Gmail API (`users.messages.send`).

---

### Segment 5: Outbox (Sent Communications Ledger) (3:30 – 4:05)
- **Sent History:** Navigate to `/outbox` showing all dispatched candidate replies.
- **Preview Modal:** Open "View Reply" modal displaying full recipient details, timestamps, intent category, and formatted body.
- **Thread Deep-Link:** Click "Thread →" to navigate directly back to the active conversation thread in the Inbox.

---

### Segment 6: HR Productivity Tools (4:05 – 4:45)
- **Attendance Pattern Checker (`/attendance`):**
  - Upload timesheet CSV; show anomaly detection engine flagging Overtime (>12h), Missing Hours, Consecutive Leave, Weekend Work, and Inconsistent Records.
  - Review one-sentence natural language explanations and severity tagging (`HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, `LOW`).
  - Demonstrate PostgreSQL persistence across page reloads and single/bulk deletion.
- **Hiring Board Stall Alerter (`/stall-alerter`):**
  - Show candidate pipeline tracking with configurable stall thresholds (3 to 14 days).
  - Review urgency grouping (`Critical`, `High`, `Medium`) and stage-specific recruiter guidance.

---

### Segment 7: Settings & Data Management (4:45 – 5:15)
- **Settings:** Show global rows-per-page preferences.
- **Data Management:** Explain the difference between Soft Candidate Data Clear (preserves OAuth credentials) and Full Factory Reset (wipes all 11 database tables).
- **Wrap-up:** Reiterate live production links on Render and GitHub repository.
